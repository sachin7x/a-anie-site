How it works

Three panels. No telepathy.

01

Speak like a person.

Not like a dictation machine. Use the filler words you already use, restart mid-sentence, switch languages halfway through, name your friends however you actually say their names.

02

It edits while you talk.

An ordinary speech model transcribes what you said. A-Anie rewrites what you meant — applying tone, removing filler, merging repetition, resolving self-correction, picking the right punctuation for the cadence.

filler removed · repetition merged · punctuation added
03

Lands in any text field.

A-Anie writes the cleaned text at the current cursor position — no app switch, no clipboard detour, no "save then paste" round trip. Whatever app you opened, that's where it lands.

Questions you might already be asking.

Does it work in every app I have open?

Yes. A-Anie writes into whatever text field currently has focus — web apps, native apps, terminal emulators. No per-app integration required.

How is this different from my OS dictation key?

OS dictation (iOS, Android, Windows Speech Recognition) transcribes what you said literally. A-Anie edits while you talk — removing filler, resolving self-corrections, merging repeated words, adding structure.

Does it understand Indian English?

Yes — that's the entire reason A-Anie exists.

What about regional Indian languages?

Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu are first-class, plus English. A-Anie also handles Hinglish and code-switching between Hindi and English without losing meaning.

What microphone do I need?

Anything your OS recognises. The built-in laptop mic is fine for short bursts; a clip-on lavalier helps for long sessions.

Is it noisy-environment friendly?

It handles moderate background noise cleanly. For loud cafés and open-plan offices, a directional headset mic works better than the built-in one.

Where does my audio go?

Audio is sent to the transcription service for the duration of the session, then discarded. Transcripts are stored against your account. See our privacy panel above.

Is the free tier really free?

Yes. No card, no trial, no email gate. Limits exist; they're listed on the pricing page.

What happens if the solo developer goes offline?

Honestly: nothing magical. A-Anie is small enough that I can publish a roadmap and a maintenance plan; you'll get weeks of warning, not a midnight shutdown.
