How pricing will be set.

A-Anie is a solo-built product with a single set of real costs: speech-to-text inference, transcript storage, and the time of one developer. Pricing will be a function of those costs, not of investor expectations or competitor markup.

When the early-access period ends, this page will publish:

  • The exact monthly price for individual use.
  • The free-tier limit (in minutes per month) and what changes when you exceed it.
  • The conditions under which a free account can keep working without a card on file.
  • Team, education, and non-profit pricing — only if and when those plans exist.

Until then, every claim on this page would be a guess. We're not going to write a guess and then defend it.
