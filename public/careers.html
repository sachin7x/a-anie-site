Careers.

We are a team of one. There is no careers page in the conventional sense — only this one, with a real answer in it.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The short answer

A-Anie is designed, built, supported, and shipped by Sachin Dohdiya, working alone from Kishangarh. There are no open roles, no recruiters, no interview pipeline, and no HR contact. The “careers” tab on a real company's site links to a job board; here, it links to this page, and this page is the job board.

Why this page exists anyway

Most information-architecture patterns in the voice-dictation space include a careers page. Wispr Flow's site has one. A credible one-person product should still answer the URL — visitors reaching /careers from a link, a sitemap, or a security review should not find a 404 or, worse, a stub that pretends a team exists. The honest answer is short, but it is the answer.

If you wanted to work on A-Anie anyway

There is no formal application path. Two things that do happen:

  • Open-source contributions are welcome on the parts of the stack that are public (the marketing site, the demo bundle, the documentation). Email before you start a large piece of work so the developer can sanity-check the direction.
  • Hiring eventually — if the product grows to the point where one person is the bottleneck, the first hire will be visible on this page. The page will be replaced with a real job board at that point; it will not be replaced quietly.

What will change this page

This page is deleted the day A-Anie hires its first non-founding contributor. The first edit will be the first job listing, the second will be the founder's note about why the team is bigger than one. Neither edit will happen before that day is real.
