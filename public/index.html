How A-Anie works

Speak like a person. It edits while you talk. Lands in any text field.

01
Speak like a person.

Not like a dictation machine. Use the filler words you already use, restart mid-sentence, switch languages halfway through.

02
It edits while you talk.

An ordinary speech model transcribes what you said. A-Anie rewrites what you meant — tone, filler, repetition, punctuation, cadence.

03
Lands in any text field.

No app switch, no clipboard detour. Whatever app you opened, that's where it lands.

The realization

There was a period when most of my waking hours went through a keyboard. Messages. Code. Documents. Long explanations to friends, longer explanations to clients. My hands were always on the keys, even when I was sitting across from someone I cared about.

So I asked the obvious question

Why am I typing thoughts I could simply say?

So now I don't have to type

I just have to speak. A-Anie catches what I mean, cleans it up, and writes it where my cursor already is — Gmail, Slack, Notion, VS Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, WhatsApp, Obsidian.
