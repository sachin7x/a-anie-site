What we store. What we don't. How to delete it.

A-Anie is a solo-built product. There is no legal department, no compliance team, and no certifications claimed. This page is a plain-language explanation of what data the service holds against your account and what it doesn't.

1. Audio

What you say is sent to the speech-to-text service for the duration of the session. After processing, the audio is not stored.

  • Retention: Discarded after the session ends.
  • Used for: Speech-to-text processing only.
  • Used to train models: No.

2. Transcripts

The edited text output is stored against your account if you are signed in. This is the default behaviour so that you can scroll back through your dictation history.

  • Retention: Until you delete the transcript or close your account.
  • Used for: Showing your history in the product.
  • Shared with third parties: No, except as required by law.

3. Account data

When you create an account, A-Anie stores the minimum needed to identify you: an email address, a password hash (argon2id — never the plaintext), and a display name if you provided one. Optionally, a usage counter.

Field Why we store it Encrypted at rest
Email Sign-in and account recovery Yes (at rest)
Password Authentication argon2id hash, never plaintext
Display name Personalisation, if you set one Yes
Account creation date Audit Yes

4. Telemetry and errors

A-Anie uses Sentry for error tracking and OpenTelemetry for tracing. These services receive:

  • Error stack traces (when something crashes)
  • Request traces (with trace IDs, not your dictated content)
  • Browser session metadata (browser version, OS version, no IP-to-identity mapping)

Dictated content is never included in error or trace payloads. If a session crashes mid-dictation, only the metadata of the request is recorded — not the audio or the text.

5. Cookies and storage

The marketing site uses no tracking cookies. If you sign in to the product, a session token is stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in across page reloads.

  • No advertising cookies.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • One authentication token, stored locally.

6. Third-party services used

Currently, A-Anie's data flow touches the following external services:

Service Purpose What they receive
Speech-to-text provider Transcription Audio stream (discarded after session)
Sentry Error tracking Stack traces, session metadata
OpenTelemetry collector Tracing Request metadata
Hosting provider Service hosting Standard server access

7. What we don't claim

A-Anie does not currently claim any of the following. If your use case requires them, please ask before relying on the service.

  • SOC 2 — not claimed
  • ISO 27001 — not claimed
  • HIPAA compliance — not claimed
  • GDPR certification — not claimed (GDPR principles are followed; certification is a separate audit we have not undertaken)
  • End-to-end encryption of dictated content — the audio stream is encrypted in transit (TLS); content is not stored after processing
If you need a formal answer for procurement: Use the contact form. The developer will answer honestly, including if the answer is "we don't have that yet."

8. Your controls

You can do the following at any time:

  • View your account data on the profile page (link inside the desktop app)
  • Delete individual transcripts from your history
  • Export your data in a portable format (planned — currently via the contact form)
  • Close your account — see the actions below
Close your account

Closing your account deletes your email, display name, password hash, sessions, and stored transcripts from the production database within 7 days. Backups are rotated within 30 days.

Until an in-app control is shipped, request account closure by sending a message from the email address on the account.
